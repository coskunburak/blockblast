# App Store Privacy Texts

## Tracking usage description

NSUserTrackingUsageDescription (already configured in build settings):

> We use your identifier to measure ad performance and keep ads relevant.

## Data handling summary

- Core gameplay data (score, turns, combos) is used for in-app progression and analytics.
- Ad-related events (impressions, rewarded grants) are used for monetization balancing.
- Purchase status is stored to restore entitlements (Remove Ads, Starter Pack).
- Tracking consent and ATT status are explicitly requested and persisted.

## User-facing privacy commitments

- Rewarded ads remain optional.
- Personalized ads are gated behind consent + ATT authorization.
- Non-personalized operation is supported when tracking is denied.
